Why go digital

From walk-in nursery to an online-first business

Today the nursery sells only to customers who visit in person. This platform opens a second storefront that's open 24×7, reaches customers across the city and beyond, and makes daily operations easier.

What we're building

Three applications, one platform

Website, app and dashboard share one backend and database. A product added once appears everywhere instantly.

Customer journey

From discovery to doorstep in six steps

Designed for mobile-first Indian shoppers: OTP login instead of passwords, UPI in one tap, and order updates on WhatsApp.

Discover

Finds the nursery on Google, Instagram or WhatsApp, or opens the app

Browse

Filters by category, sunlight, pot size or occasion; reads care tips

Add to cart

Picks quantity, checks delivery for their pin code, applies a festival coupon

Pay

UPI, card, net banking or Cash on Delivery, secured by Razorpay

Track

Gets SMS, WhatsApp & push updates: confirmed → dispatched → delivered

Return

Watering reminders, reviews, wishlist alerts and one-tap reorder
