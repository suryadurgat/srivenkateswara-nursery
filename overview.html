01Project summary

The nursery currently sells to walk-in customers only. This project delivers a responsive website, a cross-platform mobile app (Android & iOS) and an admin dashboard, all powered by one shared backend. Customers can browse, order, pay online or by cash on delivery, and get home delivery. The owner can manage products, stock, orders, bulk event quotes and festival promotions from one place.

09Key workflows

Online payment (Razorpay)

CheckoutCustomer confirms cart, address & delivery date
Create orderServer re-prices the cart, reserves stock, creates a Razorpay order
PayRazorpay Checkout handles UPI, card or net banking
VerifyServer verifies the payment signature; the webhook confirms it
ConfirmOrder marked “Paid · New” and stock committed
NotifyEmail, SMS, WhatsApp & push sent to the customer; owner alerted

Order status lifecycle

New→Processing→Dispatched→Delivered

An order can be Cancelled before dispatch, and cancelling puts the stock back. Online payments are refunded through Razorpay. Every status change notifies the customer automatically.

Cash on Delivery

COD orders skip the payment step and carry a paymentMode=COD, paid=false flag. The delivery person marks the order collected, which sets paid=true. To limit risk, COD is capped (₹5,000 in the prototype), can be turned off per pin code, and asks for OTP confirmation on first orders.

Bulk / event quote

Customer submits a request → admin is alerted → admin prepares the quote (unit price, tier discount, setup charges) → quote sent by WhatsApp & email with a payment link for the advance → once the advance is paid the order is confirmed and delivery is scheduled for the function date.

Festival campaign

Admin creates a campaign with dates, discount, coupon, banner and product collection → it switches on and off automatically on schedule → the homepage banner, sale prices and coupon go live together → a push notification and email go out to opted-in customers.
